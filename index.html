03 — ABOUT THE DIRECTOR

BEHIND THE FRAME

A LIFE
IN FRAMES.

CD.

Claudio D’Avascio is an Italian filmmaker and director working across film, television and digital media.

In 2015, he directed the RAI television miniseries L’Ispettore Coliandro – Il ritorno, a parallel project developed alongside the television series directed by the Manetti Bros. In 2017, he directed the noir feature film Il Manutentore (The Habit), recognized at the 71st Salerno International Film Festival.

His recent films EVA 01, THE WHITE FLY and THE ORDINARY PSYCHOS explore identity, consciousness and the unsettling side of everyday life, combining traditional cinematic storytelling with new possibilities in generative filmmaking.
